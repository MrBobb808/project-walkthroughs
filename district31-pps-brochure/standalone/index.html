Who is a Pupil Personnel Services (PPS) Administrator?

A PPS Administrator provides leadership and oversight for student support services to promote equity and compliance.

(PPS) Position
Responsibilities

  • Provides visionary leadership for all student support services throughout the district.
  • Analyzes data to improve student outcomes.
  • Promotes Multi-Tiered Systems of Support (MTSS).
  • Works collaboratively with principals, teachers, families, and community agencies, and coordinates with special education stakeholders to remove barriers to student success.
  • Develops programs that promote equity, compliance, and inclusion.
  • Supervises attendance programs and community partnerships so every student is set up for success.
  • Oversees crisis response and intervention.
