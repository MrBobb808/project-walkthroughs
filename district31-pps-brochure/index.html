Who is a Pupil Personnel Services Administrator (PPS)?

A PPS Administrator provides leadership and oversight for student support services to promote equity and compliance.

Responsibilities

  • PPS Director provides visionary leadership for all student support services throughout the district.
  • Analyze data to improve student outcomes.
  • Promote Multi-Tiered Systems of Support (MTSS).
  • Work collaboratively with principals, teachers, families, community agencies, and Special Education stakeholders to remove barriers to student success.
  • Develop programs that promote equity, compliance, and inclusion.
  • Supervise Attendance Programs and Community Partnerships.
  • Oversee crisis response and intervention.
